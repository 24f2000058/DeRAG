Recent case-control analysis of IgG antibody prevalence to Helicobacter pylori in gastric cancer patients found infection to be protective rather than causative: the odds ratio for gastric cancer risk in H pylori-positive individuals was 0.36 (95% confidence interval 0.21 to 0.61), indicating H pylori colonization lowers gastric cancer risk by suppressing more aggressive carcinogenic strains of gut bacteria.
